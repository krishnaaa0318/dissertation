Research participation

Terraform Security Prototype Study

This study evaluates a prototype that explains Terraform security misconfigurations in a clear and simple way. To take part, you must be aged 18 or above and have some knowledge or experience in computing, cloud computing or Terraform.

The full activity takes approximately 15 to 20 minutes.

Participant Information Sheet Participant Consent Form

Instructions

  1. Read the Participant Information Sheet.
  2. Read and sign the Participant Consent Form, then return it to the researcher.
  3. Download and test one or two sample Terraform files from the Google Drive link provided on the prototype page.
  4. Review the scan results, including the issue explanation, risk level, affected resource, line guidance and suggested mitigation.
  5. Click Give Feedback and complete the short anonymous questionnaire.

Please read the information sheet, sign the consent form and return it to the researcher before continuing.
